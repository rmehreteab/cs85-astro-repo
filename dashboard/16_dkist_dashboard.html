Four things to notice (click "Show me")

Embargo (locked)

When scientists win telescope time, they get their data to themselves for a while so they can publish first. After the embargo ends, anyone in the world can download it for free.

Seeing

How steady the air above the telescope was, like the shimmer over a hot road. It's measured as r₀: the size of a patch of calm air. Bigger is sharper. Here, Fair is 5 cm or more, Good is 7 cm or more, and Excellent is 10 cm or more.

Wavelength

The color of light the camera recorded. Each color comes from particular atoms at a particular height in the Sun's atmosphere, so choosing a color means choosing which layer to look at. Many of DKIST's colors are infrared, invisible to our eyes.

Data: NSO DKIST Data Center, dkist.data.nso.edu, searched with the dkist Python package. Preview movies are hosted by the DKIST Data Center on Vimeo. Built for CS85 project 16.
