When embargoed data become public

Each bar counts datasets whose embargo ends that month. During the embargo, only the observing team can download them.

Every dataset: date and wavelength

Filled marks are public, open marks are embargoed. Each instrument has its own color and shape. Wavelength is on a log scale.

Seeing quality

r₀ (the Fried parameter) is the size of a patch of steady air above the telescope. Bigger r₀ means steadier air and sharper images. The dashed line is your minimum.
